How this works
Day strip — tap a day (Mon–Fri; weekend days only appear if something is booked; 🏖 = bank holiday). Jobs are in driving order from the workshop with drive times; use the ▲▼ buttons to reorder stops — the route times update and the order is saved for everyone.
Card colours — green = install, blue = team delivery, teal = Andy, amber = courier, grey = collection, dashed violet = survey/amendment visit. Team jobs only hides everything that isn't the crew's own drive (installs + team deliveries + visits).
🗺 Directions opens maps with the address set. 📞 dials. 📁 Project folder opens the customer's OneDrive (drawings in "3. Drawings").
📝 Job report is required for every install/team delivery — arrival, time taken, QC4, problems. Ticking rework required emails the office immediately.
