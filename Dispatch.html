How dispatch planning works
Each card is one shed (a multi-shed order can go to different sites on different days, so each shed is planned on its own). The 🎯 badge is the target dispatch date sales gave the customer. Dragging a card onto a day sets your proposed dispatch date.
⏸ ON HOLD = sales set the shed's Dispatch status to "On hold" in HubSpot (its dispatch date is blank on purpose). It's left out of suggested install days; the badge clears when sales lift the hold.
Click a week header to expand that week into full-height day columns — same drag-and-drop, plus a notes box per day. Busy weeks widen automatically on the board. £ figures = dispatch value for the day/week (the shed's HubSpot "dispatch value" field, split equally across an order's sheds).
Card colour = dispatch type: white = Install · blue = team delivery · amber = courier · grey = collection · dashed violet = survey/amendment visits. The left border stays the status colour.
➕ Join suggestions in Suggest install days: drafts that fit onto a day already proposed/confirmed with the customer (spare van space, same area, inside their ±2-week window). 🔒 stops are the agreed jobs — "Add to day" only places the new ones.
Status flow: draft internal only → proposed offered to the client → confirmed agreed (card locks 🔒; unconfirm via the card to move it). Change status by clicking a card.
Red outline = proposed date is more than 2 weeks from the target — outside what the customer was told.
⚠ No colour = this shed has no finish chosen yet; you can place it as a draft, but can't propose it to the client until the colour is set in HubSpot (the spray booth plans by colour).
✨ Suggest install days plans whole days: real drive times from the workshop, van space (4 small-shed slots), ≤2–3 customers, ≤1h between addresses, ≤10h door-to-door, London runs on Tue–Thu only. Each suggestion shows the route with leg times — "Place day" books all its orders as drafts. Flag a big job as Single-install day in its card to give it the van to itself.
🔒 on a card = its status is set by sales in HubSpot (shed dispatch_status = Proposed/Confirmed) — change it there, then Refresh. A red 🔒 = the confirmed date is within 14 days; moving it now costs the customer a penalty (details on the card's modal).
Dashed violet cards = amendment 🔧 and site-survey 📋 visits from the HubSpot Amendments pipeline, shown on their agreed dates with the order's original dispatch date. They're read-only here — reschedule them in HubSpot (click the card → Open in HubSpot). Ones with no date yet sit at the bottom of the left tray.
Grey ✓ dispatched cards appear when you scroll into past weeks (← Prev): read-only history of what actually shipped, on the day it was planned (hover shows the date it was ticked dispatched if different). Past day/week £ totals include them.
