How the tracker works
Tap a job row to open it. Tap stage buttons in order to mark them done — green = done, blue = dispatched. Tapping a done stage again un-does it (you'll be asked to confirm).
QC stages ask for faults when completed — Cancel just closes; Save records the text; Clear faults removes it.
Date badge = install date: red = overdue, amber = within 2 weeks. ⏸ ON HOLD = sales parked the shed in HubSpot (Dispatch status "On hold"); it sits in its own section until the hold is lifted.
Notes and dates save automatically — watch for "✓ Saved" at the bottom of the job.
Odd Jobs (right column) are batch tasks — ply prep, lid frames, base frames — ticked off per shed, separate from the build stages.
