How this board works
Cards are sheds to build. Drag them from "To Schedule" onto a week. The coloured stripe is the paint finish.
✎ Edit (or double-click a card): set a build-time override in HOURS, planning notes, or lock the job to its week (shown as 🔒). Build-time precedence everywhere: your override > HubSpot estimated_build_hours > standard time.
Date badge = the date production is working towards (confirmed/proposed dispatch date, else the sales target): green = comfortable, amber = within 2 weeks, red = overdue.
DRAFT / PROPOSED / CONFIRMED pills show the dispatch-date status from the Dispatch Planner.
Dots = the 10 production stages completed (from the Tracker).
BESPOKE = no standard build time; it counts as 0 in capacity until you set hours via ✎ (or estimated_build_hours on the shed in HubSpot).
⏸ ON HOLD = sales set the shed's Dispatch status to "On hold" in HubSpot. It's skipped by Plan sprays; the badge clears when sales lift the hold.
Add-on icons (hover a card's icons for names): 🔒 Security Pack · 🚪 Bi-folds · 🌿 Green Roof · 🔗 Joining Panel · 🪝 Storage Hooks · 🗄️ End Shelves · ♻️ Recycling Shelf · 🚲 Steadyracks · 📦 Additional Storage · 📝 Additional requirements.
Capacity = man-hours booked (build-days × 6.5h) vs the team's available hours that week, already deducting booked leave (red name pills). Finish batching (click to expand) = which colours are booked this week.
Spray-day lanes: each week splits into Mon–Fri. Drag a card onto a day to set its spray date — assembly is auto-set 2 working days later, and the 🎨 chip's tooltip shows when framing must start. The per-day meter targets 2.5–3 sprayed (max 4). Jobs in the top "no spray day yet" zone are in the week but unplanned.
🎨 Plan sprays auto-fills the week: same colours land on the same day (fewer booth changeovers), 3 per day, spilling to 4 only when full.
⚠ mixed on a day = 3+ jobs sprayed that day in more than one colour — the count looks fine but it costs extra booth changeovers. Worth reshuffling if you can.
Click a week header for the day-by-day view (framing / spraying / assembling per day).
⛶ Focus week shows just one week at full width instead of four narrow columns — the "To Schedule" list stays put on the left. Click again for the 4-week overview.
