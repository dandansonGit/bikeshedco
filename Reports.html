How these numbers work
Produced £ — every stage tick "earns" its share of the job's product value (DRAFT hour-share weights, Steve + Dan to review). Product value = HubSpot shed value, or derived from the deal's line items (net of VAT, dispatch lines excluded) for single-shed deals.
Combined £ — produced + dispatch service value (added at reporting; no separate install-crew target, per the board's decision).
Rev/sold hr = produced ÷ estimated hours of the work done (pricing lens). Rev/paid hr = value ÷ paid hours (workshop: 9 × 8h × workdays − leave; combined adds the install crew's 82 hrs/wk). Utilisation = sold hours ÷ paid hours.
Coverage warnings matter: jobs without a value or hours estimate earn £0/0h here — fix the data, not the formula.
